How it works

For sellers

  1. Create a free seller account and add your bank payout details.
  2. List your products — price, quantity, and origin.
  3. When a buyer orders, we hold the funds securely.
  4. Ship the order and mark it as shipped with tracking info.
  5. Once the buyer confirms receipt, your payout is released — minus a 5% commission.

For buyers

  1. Browse verified listings from African farmers and processors.
  2. Add products to your cart and check out by card or bank transfer.
  3. We hold your payment until you confirm the order arrived.
  4. Track shipping status directly from your dashboard.
  5. Raise a dispute any time if something isn't right.
